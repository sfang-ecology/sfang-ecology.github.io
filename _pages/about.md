I am currently a researcher in ecology, with interests in forest dynamics, plant functional traits, biodiversity, and environmental change.

My work combines ecological field observations, statistical analysis, and computational methods to understand how plants and ecosystems respond to environmental variation.

## Research interests

- Forest ecology and forest dynamics

- Plant functional traits

- Biodiversity and ecosystem functioning

- Climate change ecology

- Ecological data analysis

- Remote sensing and spatial ecology
